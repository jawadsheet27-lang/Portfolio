Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

XARV is a one-person studio. This page explains, in plain language, what information we handle and why.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us: your name, business details, and messages when you contact us on WhatsApp, email, or Instagram. If Google Analytics is enabled, anonymous usage statistics (pages visited, device type) may be collected.

How we use it

To reply to you, prepare quotes, and deliver your project. That's it. We don't run ads on your data, build profiles, or share your details with anyone.

What we never do

We never sell, rent, or trade your information. We never add you to marketing lists you didn't ask for.

Third-party services

Conversations happen on WhatsApp, email, or Instagram — their own privacy policies apply. This site is hosted on Vercel.

Questions?

Message us anytime on WhatsApp or email and we'll answer within 24 hours.
